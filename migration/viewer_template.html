Storms are the risks that bring the rain; the tree is the response that gives shelter and fruit. Each storm cloud above the tree sits over the leaves whose responses carry that risk, sized by how many responses in view do, and rains on them; select a cloud to see the families the literature ties to it. Economy grows on the left, people in the middle, governance on the right. Under each RHDR subpillar (its code at the fork) the twigs are the framework families, each from a published framework, named at their fork; the leaves are our families, sized by the responses on them; the buds along each twig are the levers, what a government does, gold where responses sit. The dashed branch beside each pillar holds the responses that fit no family.
