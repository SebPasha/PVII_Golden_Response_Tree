PVII Key Messages

Each message is one chain from a country's own plan. It runs from what the country faces and what it is good at, through the mechanism (a lever on the tree), to the objective it serves (Growth, Wellbeing or Governance, the RHDR pillars). Then come the instrument the state uses and the people it is for. Pick a country.

ContextMegatrend risks and opportunities, domestic pressures, comparative advantages
MechanismLever on tree v4, or its family
ObjectiveGrowth, Wellbeing or Governance
InstrumentMoney, Authority, Information, Organisation
TargetThe groups of people it is for
How the messages are built
  • For each country the page takes its latest national plan with at least 30 strategies (else its latest long-term vision), as on the explorer page. Brazil's two most recent plans, the PPA 2020-2023 (Bolsonaro) and PPA 2024-2027 (Lula), were both run again through the current workflow (steps C, E, F, M) so that they are read the same way.
  • The strategies are grouped by mechanism family on tree v4 and weighted by how often the plan mentions them. The national budget lines placed on the same family are added for each budget year.
  • One gpt-5.4 call per plan (step K) writes four to six messages from those families. It may only use the families, levers, instruments and groups it is given; the page drops any lever or family it invents. Comparative advantages and opportunities come from the strategies, and for the two Brazil plans also from what the plan document itself says (step C).
  • A message's weight is the share of the plan's mentions in the families it joins.
  • Gaps flag what is missing: a megatrend named but not answered, only Information instruments where money or rules are needed, a priority with no budget, no group named.
