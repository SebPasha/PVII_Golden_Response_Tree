The Mother Tree, version 4

Storms are the risks that bring the rain; the tree is the response that gives shelter and fruit. Economy grows on the left, people in the middle, governance on the right. Leaves are the 60 families; the buds along each twig are the levers, what a government does, where every strategy is placed. The pathways under a lever are the literature it works through.
