# Mother tree v1: validation

Built 2026-10-20. 57 families, 259 mechanisms, 446 objectives after consolidation (from 456), 9918 strategies in 45 countries.

## Coverage, both directions

- Objective side: 456 of 456 source objectives placed exactly once (exclusive and exhaustive).
- Strategy side: 9918 of 9918 strategies resolve to a node.
- Literature side: 221 mechanisms hold strategies; 38 are empty nodes, kept with their literature (empty_nodes.csv).
- Every mechanism passes the structural form of the five-question admission test (259 of 259): named as a relationship, input, transmission and outcome stated, a citation present, a sibling distinction stated, a family process stated. That is not evidence that each paper supports the claim. Source verification is tracked separately below.

## Source verification

- Evidence: 2 mechanisms source-verified, 257 cited but not yet independently verified.
- Risk tags: 231 tagged from the review notes and not yet source-verified; 14 baseline mechanisms with no risk tag by design.
- Conditional risk links: 11 accepted as conditional. They apply to a strategy only when its wording meets the stated scope, and never enter the mechanism × risk matrix. 3 were rejected.
- Strategies carrying a conditional risk link: 2.

## Diagnostic against the earlier RHDR coding

On the 6826 strategies whose earlier HDICategory maps onto an amended subpillar, the tree's primary subpillar agrees on 2635 (39%). Agreement is a diagnostic, not a target: the earlier coding was by strategy and by the old framework, the tree is by mechanism.

## Second coder and sibling test

A second coder placed a stratified 10% sample (45 objectives) blind, from the family and mechanism definitions only. First pass against coder 2: same family 39 (87%), same node 34 (76%). Of the 11 disagreements, 9 were resolved toward coder 2 and 2 kept with a reason (ADJUDICATION.md). Agreement after adjudication is not a validation statistic; the first-pass figures are.

Sibling test: every L3 objective is checked for a sibling mechanism that its own wording also matches. 25 were challenged and kept with a reason; none is unadjudicated.
