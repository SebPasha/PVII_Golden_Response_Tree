# Biodiversity Loss: literature review note

## Reference model

Inclusive wealth (Dasgupta 2021) treats natural capital as an asset with a shadow price; open access drives it below its efficient level (Gordon 1954) unless rules, rights or incentives bind (Ostrom 1990; Costello, Gaines and Lynham 2008).

**Levers the model names.** Stock levels; property and harvest rules; habitat protection; payments that align local incentives.

**External-validity check.** Protection without local incentives leaks; harvest growth without rights runs the stock down.
