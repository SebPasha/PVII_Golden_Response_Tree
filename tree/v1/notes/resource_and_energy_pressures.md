# Resource and Energy Pressures: literature review note

## Reference model

Resource economics: rents should be reinvested to keep wealth from falling (Hartwick 1977); booms cause Dutch disease and rent-seeking (Corden and Neary 1982; Sachs and Warner 1995; Mehlum, Moene and Torvik 2006); energy and water security are diversification and allocation problems.

**Levers the model names.** Rent capture and reinvestment; supply reliability and pricing; storage and allocation of water; food system productivity.

**External-validity check.** Extraction without a reinvestment rule shifts costs to future generations; subsidies without cost recovery hollow out utilities.
