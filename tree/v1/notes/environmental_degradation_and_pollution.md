# Environmental Degradation and Pollution: literature review note

## Reference model

The environmental Kuznets curve (Grossman and Krueger 1995) and its critics (Stern 2004): pollution falls with income only where regulation binds and enforcement works; exposure harms health and human capital (Chay and Greenstone 2003; Ebenstein et al. 2017).

**Levers the model names.** Standards and prices; enforcement; composition and technique of production; waste flows; exposure of children.

**External-validity check.** Standards without enforcement capacity do not bind; the review links this note to Regulatory enforcement capacity.
