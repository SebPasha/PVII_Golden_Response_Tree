# Democratic Backsliding: literature review note

## Reference model

Autocratisation waves (Luhrmann and Lindberg 2019; V-Dem) run through executive aggrandisement: incremental capture of courts, legislatures, media and electoral bodies (Levitsky and Ziblatt 2018). Accountability works through judicial review, oversight, audits, information and electoral competition.

**Levers the model names.** Judicial independence; legislative oversight; audit; media independence; electoral integrity; civic space.

**External-validity check.** Anti-corruption without independent courts and media is easily reversed.
