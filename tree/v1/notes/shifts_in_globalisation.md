# Shifts in Globalisation: literature review note

## Reference model

Five strands: comparative advantage and capability dynamics (Hausmann, Hwang and Rodrik 2007; Hidalgo and Hausmann 2009); value chains (Baldwin 2016; World Bank 2020); trade shocks and slow adjustment (Autor, Dorn and Hanson 2013; Dix-Carneiro and Kovak 2017); external finance and buffers (Calvo 1998; Rey 2015); geoeconomics (Farrell and Newman 2019; Baldwin and Freeman 2022; Juhasz, Lane and Rodrik 2023). Migration and remittances (Yang 2008; Docquier and Rapoport 2012) are the labour side.

**Levers the model names.** Export complexity; trade costs; market access; FDI linkages; buffers; supplier concentration; adjustment speed.

**External-validity check.** Promoting exports without upgrading or trade-cost reduction is thin; opening without an adjustment family is incomplete.
