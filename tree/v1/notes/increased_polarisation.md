# Increased Polarisation: literature review note

## Reference model

Polarisation measures (Esteban and Ray 1994) and horizontal inequality (Stewart 2008; Cederman, Weidmann and Gleditsch 2011) tie group inequality to conflict; affective polarisation (Iyengar et al. 2019) runs through identity and media; contact, recognition and reconciliation reverse it (Mousa 2020; Cilliers, Dube and Siddiqi 2016).

**Levers the model names.** Between-group inequality; contact across groups; shared identity; reconciliation; information environment.

**External-validity check.** Cohesion programmes without addressing group inequality treat symptoms.
