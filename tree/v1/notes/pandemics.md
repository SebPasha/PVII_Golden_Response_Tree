# Pandemics: literature review note

## Reference model

Epidemic macroeconomics (Eichenbaum, Rebelo and Trabandt 2021) puts a trade-off between health and activity at the centre; surveillance, surge capacity and income protection are the levers that shift it, and private behaviour responds to risk before policy does (Goolsbee and Syverson 2021).

**Levers the model names.** Detection lead time; surge capacity; supply security for health goods; continuity of services; income protection during suppression.

**External-validity check.** Preparedness without income protection makes suppression politically fragile; surge without surveillance arrives late.
