# Demographic Shifts: literature review note

## Reference model

The demographic dividend: output per person equals output per worker times the support ratio (Bloom and Williamson 1998; Mason and Lee, NTA). The identity organises the note but is not a node. The mechanisms are fertility control, female and older-worker participation, human capital per child and lifecycle saving.

**Levers the model names.** Fertility; female and older participation; schooling per child; lifecycle saving; old-age income and care.

**External-validity check.** Read against the country's dividend stage: a pre-dividend country without fertility and girls' schooling channels misses the first dividend; a post-dividend country without pensions and care misses the second.
