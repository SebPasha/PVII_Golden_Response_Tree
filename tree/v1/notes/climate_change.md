# Climate Change: literature review note

## Reference model

Unbreakable (Hallegatte, Vogt-Schilb, Bangalore and Rozenberg 2017) separates asset losses from well-being losses: the poor lose a larger share of what they own and recover more slowly, so protecting assets, raising coping capacity and speeding recovery each cut losses by different routes. Mitigation is a global public-good problem (Nordhaus 2008), solved by prices, directed innovation and avoiding lock-in.

**Levers the model names.** Hazard exposure and vulnerability; coping capacity; speed of recovery; the carbon price and the direction of technical change; the lifetime of capital.

**External-validity check.** A plan that adapts without addressing the coping capacity of the poor understates losses; a mitigation response with no price or innovation channel relies on luck.
