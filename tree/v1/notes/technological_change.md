# Technological Change: literature review note

## Reference model

Task-based automation (Acemoglu and Restrepo 2019) displaces labour from tasks and reinstates it in new ones; diffusion and absorptive capacity (Comin and Mestieri 2018; Cirera and Maloney 2017) decide whether frontier technology reaches firms; digital infrastructure is a public good whose returns depend on skills (Akerman, Gaarder and Mogstad 2015; Hjort and Poulsen 2019).

**Levers the model names.** Adoption lags; skills complementarity; connectivity; data and market rules; direction of AI.

**External-validity check.** Connectivity without skills and adoption support leaves returns unrealised; adoption without adjustment support concentrates losses.
