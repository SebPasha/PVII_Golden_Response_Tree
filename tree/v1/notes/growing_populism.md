# Growing Populism: literature review note

## Reference model

Economic insecurity and backlash (Guriev and Papaioannou 2022; Rodrik 2018): losers from trade and automation, places left behind (Rodriguez-Pose 2018), and falling trust in institutions (Algan et al. 2017) raise demand for populists.

**Levers the model names.** Regional equity; adjustment of displaced workers; cost-of-living protection; trust through service delivery; social dialogue.

**External-validity check.** Populism is mostly addressed through other families; a plan with no regional-equity or adjustment channel leaves the demand side untouched.
