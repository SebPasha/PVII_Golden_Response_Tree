# Mother tree v1 reference (families L2 and mechanisms L3)

Holding nodes: H-SPE Standard People (culture, heritage, sport, religious, traditional services); H-SEC Standard Economy (routine sector infrastructure with no mechanism); H-SGO Standard Governance (defence, national/border security, diplomacy, consular, government facilities, government communication); H-GOAL Goals held outside the tree (outcome-shaped objectives such as reduce poverty, accelerate growth).

## L2-01 Group-based barriers and discrimination  [Tackling Structural Exclusion]
Barriers that exclude people because of the group they belong to: discrimination, norms, missing documents, disability-related access and unequal voice.
Includes: Objectives about gender equality, social inclusion, disability, care burdens, legal identity and digital divides by group.
Excludes: Excludes violence and coercion (Violence, coercion and exploitation) and group conflict (Horizontal inequality and social cohesion).
- L3-001 Taste-based and statistical discrimination: group identity in hiring, lending and services -> prejudice or group averages lower access -> gaps in employment, pay and services
- L3-002 Gender norms and intra-household bargaining: women's control of resources -> norms and bargaining power decide allocation within households -> women's and children's outcomes
- L3-003 Political representation of excluded groups: reserved or increased representation -> representatives allocate to their group's priorities -> public goods matching excluded groups' needs
- L3-004 Disability-related access barriers: physical, information and attitudinal barriers -> barriers limit schooling, work and services -> poverty and exclusion of disabled people
- L3-005 Unpaid care burden and female labour supply: care responsibilities -> time in unpaid care limits paid work -> female employment and earnings
- L3-006 Documentation barriers to entitlements: lack of legal identity -> no document, no access to services, finance and voting -> exclusion from services and rights

- L3-009 Child marriage and girls' human capital: age at marriage -> early marriage cuts schooling and work -> girls' education and earnings
- L3-010 Early adversity and lifetime outcomes: abuse and neglect in childhood -> trauma impairs development -> adult earnings and health

- L3-012 Polarisation and conflict intensity: concentration of a society into antagonistic groups -> polarised distributions raise the stakes of contest -> conflict intensity
- L3-013 Intergroup contact and prejudice reduction: structured contact across groups -> cooperation reduces prejudice -> tolerance and cooperation
