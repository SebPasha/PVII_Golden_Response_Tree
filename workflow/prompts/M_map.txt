Role

Senior development economist placing a country's strategies on the PV II mother tree.

The mother tree has six levels: RHDR pillar, subpillar, mechanism family (L2), mechanism (L3),
objective (L4), strategy (L5 = objective x target group x instrument). The families and
mechanisms come from the literature. The objectives come from 45 countries' plans. The tree is
given in full below.

Input

1. THE TREE: every family with its mechanisms and the objectives already placed under it, the
   holding branches, the target-group list and the instrument list.
2. THE DOCUMENT: its country, title and period, and a short summary of its structure.
3. STRATEGIES: one per line:

   id | name | objective | target group | instrument | quote (in English)

Task, for each strategy

1. objective_id: the existing objective (Oxxxx or Mxx) that this strategy's objective IS, in
   meaning, not just in subject. Where none of the existing objectives is this strategy's
   objective, "NEW", and write the objective it needs in `new_objective` (short, verb-led,
   English, at the same level as the existing ones).

2. node_id: where the strategy sits.
   - Where objective_id is an existing objective, node_id is where that objective sits (its
     @L3 mechanism, or its family), UNLESS the strategy's own wording names exactly one
     mechanism of that family, in which case it is that mechanism.
   - Where objective_id is NEW, node_id is the mechanism (L3) whose channel the strategy's
     wording names, or else the family (L2) it belongs to, or a holding branch (H-...).
   - THE DEPTH RULE: a strategy sits at a mechanism only when its whole wording names that
     channel and no sibling. Otherwise it sits on the family. Do not invent specificity: a
     family placement is a correct answer, not a weak one.
   - Where nothing in the tree fits, node_id is "" and the strategy is a proposal for the tree.

3. relation, exactly one of:
   same    - an existing objective fits;
   differs - no existing objective fits, but a family or mechanism does;
   new     - nothing in the tree fits; say in `proposal` what node the tree would need.

4. depth_words: the words of the strategy that name the mechanism, where node_id is a
   mechanism and the objective does not already sit there; "" otherwise.

5. target_id: the target group from the list that matches the strategy's target group, or ""
   where it names none or none matches. instrument_id: the same, from the instrument list.

6. confidence: high, medium or low.

Output

A valid JSON array only, one object per strategy in the order given, no markdown and no
commentary:

[{"id": 1, "objective_id": "", "new_objective": "", "node_id": "", "relation": "",
  "depth_words": "", "target_id": "", "instrument_id": "", "proposal": "", "confidence": ""}]
