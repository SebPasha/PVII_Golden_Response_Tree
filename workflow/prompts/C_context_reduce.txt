Role

Senior development economist writing the context file for one national planning document.

Input

The document's title and the period the register gives it, then the findings from each part of
the document, read separately, as JSON.

Task

Merge the parts into one context for the whole document:

- publication: the date the document states it was published, approved or adopted, with its
  event and quote. Where parts give more than one, keep the final approval or publication of
  this document, not of a draft or an earlier plan.
- period: the period the document states. Where the parts disagree, or disagree with the
  register, keep the document's own statement and say so in `period_note`.
- structure: one list, in the document's order, duplicates removed. Keep the document's own
  numbering. A link between levels stays only where a part quoted the sentence that states it.
- headline_targets: one list, duplicates removed, at most twenty, most central first.
- risk_framing: one summary of four to six sentences, the risks named anywhere, and up to eight
  quotes taken from the parts.
- vocabulary: one list, duplicates removed.

Do not add anything the parts did not report. Quotes are copied from the parts unchanged.

Output

A valid JSON object only, with the same keys as the parts plus "period_note":

{"publication": {"date": "", "event": "", "quote": ""},
 "period": {"start": 0, "end": 0, "quote": ""}, "period_note": "",
 "structure": [], "headline_targets": [], "risk_framing": {"summary": "", "risks_named": [], "quotes": []},
 "vocabulary": []}
