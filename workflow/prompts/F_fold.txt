Role

Senior development economist reading the strategy list of ONE planning document, to fold
together the rows that are the same strategy stated more than once.

Built from PV2 prompt 4b (strategy group), narrowed to one document. PV2 also merged a strategy
across successive plans; this step never does, because a strategy carried from one plan to the
next is a finding about time, and it is found later by comparing the plans on the mother tree.

Input

Strategy rows from ONE document, one per line:

  id | name | objective | target group | instrument | pillar | ref

The rows are a shard: rows that share a pillar or share words. Most of the document's rows are
not in front of you, and that is intended.

Task

Return the groups of rows that describe the SAME strategy.

MERGE when
1. Two rows describe one strategy under different names or emphasis: "Senior citizen social
   protection, care, and geriatric services" and "Senior citizen protection, care and welfare
   services" are one strategy.
2. The document states the same strategy in several chapters, or in a summary and again in the
   body.
3. One row is a narrower statement of another with the SAME objective, target group and
   instrument: merge into the broader one.

KEEP APART when
4. Two rows share a sector or vocabulary but differ in objective, target group or instrument:
   "Expand broadband to rural villages" and "Regulate cybersecurity of critical infrastructure"
   are two strategies.
5. Two rows share a target group but not an action: child protection and senior citizen care
   are two strategies even where one sentence names both.
6. You are not sure. Leaving two rows apart costs one duplicate; merging two different
   strategies loses one of them.

For each group give:
- name: the clearest of the existing names, or one at the same level that covers the group. In
  English. Never a bare sector word.
- objective, target_group, instrument: the group's three parts, taken from its rows. Where the
  rows name different target groups or instruments that all apply, join them with "; ".

Output

A valid JSON array only, listing ONLY the groups that merge. A row not named is kept as it is.
Every id appears at most once across all groups.

[{"merge_ids": [3, 17], "name": "", "objective": "", "target_group": "", "instrument": ""}]

An empty array [] is a valid answer.
